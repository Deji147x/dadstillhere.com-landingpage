# Buffer Setup — Quick Start (20 min)

## Step 1: Create Buffer Account (2 min)

1. Go to https://buffer.com
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Email: deji47.ajishe@gmail.com (or your preferred)
4. Password: [choose secure]
5. Click **Create Account**

---

## Step 2: Connect Social Profiles (5 min)

1. After signup, click **Connect a Profile**
2. Choose **LinkedIn** → Authorize → Select **Personal Profile** (not company page)
3. Click **Connect a Profile** again → **X/Twitter** → Authorize
4. Click **Connect a Profile** again → **Facebook** → Authorize → Select VoltixIO page
5. Verify all 3 show in the left sidebar under "Connected Profiles"

---

## Step 3: Bulk Import Posts (10 min)

### Method A: Copy-Paste Composer (Easiest)

1. Click **Compose** (top-left button)
2. In the text field, paste the first post (see "Posts Ready to Copy" below)
3. Click **Add link** → Paste the blog URL (e.g., `https://voltixio.com/blog/ai-scheduling-healthcare/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=week2`)
4. At the bottom, select platforms:
   - Check ✓ LinkedIn Personal (always check this first)
   - Check ✓ X/Twitter
   - Check ✓ Facebook (for some; not all posts go to all platforms)
5. Click **Schedule** (not "Share Now")
6. Pick date/time from calendar (e.g., Jul 20, 9:15 AM)
7. Click **Schedule**
8. Repeat for all 21 posts (Weeks 2-4; ~7 min total)

### Method B: CSV Import (If Available in Pro Plan)

If using Buffer Pro:
1. Download SOCIAL_30DAY_CALENDAR.md posts as CSV
2. Buffer → Settings → Import → Upload CSV
3. All posts import at once; customize dates/platforms
4. Done in 2 min

---

## Step 4: Verify Schedule (2 min)

1. Click **Calendar** (top nav)
2. Scroll to **Jul 20** → Verify 7 posts scheduled (9:15, 12:40, 3:20, etc.)
3. Scroll to **Jul 27** → Verify 7 posts scheduled
4. Scroll to **Aug 3** → Verify 7 posts scheduled
5. All dates green = **you're done**

---

## Step 5: Set Reply Reminders (1 min)

1. Click **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Under "Notifications," enable:
   - ✓ Email notifications when posts publish
   - ✓ Digest of engagements (comments/replies)
3. Save
