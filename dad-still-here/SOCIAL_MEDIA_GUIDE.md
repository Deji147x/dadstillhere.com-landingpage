## 🎨 General Social Media Guidelines

### Visual Consistency
- Always use the same color palette
- Feature the door/light motif in graphics
- Use Playfair Display for headlines
- Maintain warm, hopeful tone

### Tone & Voice
- **Warm:** Emotionally intelligent, not clinical
- **Hopeful:** Focus on connection and healing
- **Dignified:** Respect for all parties
- **Safe:** Emphasize community care and moderation

### Engagement
- Respond to all comments within 24 hours
- Like and comment on follower posts
- Share community content
- Celebrate member contributions
- Monitor mentions of related topics

### Content Calendar
Create a monthly content calendar with:
- Featured letters (first week)
- Story highlights (second week)
- Resource deep-dives (third week)
- Community reflections (fourth week)
- Weekly prompts (ongoing)

### Prohibited Content
- Never expose user identities
- Don't share private journal excerpts
- Avoid political/divisive topics
- No child-shaming or blame language
- No revenge narratives
